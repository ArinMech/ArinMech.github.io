Regulated Panel Sizing

Sized and manufactured SAE-regulated composite sandwich panels — screening 20,000+ ply schedules computationally, then validating the top candidates with physical testing.

PythonAnsys ACPCreoCLT

What

  • Built a program that iterated through 20,000+ ply schedules and shortlisted 2 to test based on stiffness and energy absorption.
  • Performed 3-point bend and perimeter shear tests on the 2 panels to obtain stress-strain curves.

How

  • Analyzed panels using fundamentals of solid mechanics and Composite Laminate Theory (CLT).
  • Wrote the ply-sweeper in Python; simulated test panels in Ansys (Static Structural + ACP).
  • Manufactured sandwich panels via layup using T700/T800 carbon fiber and an aluminum core.
  • Modeled panel locations in Creo Parametric ("Clown-Car" diagram — e.g. green = Side Impact Structure).

Results

  • Minimized weight of all regulated panels to ~22 lbs.
  • Completed the Structures Equivalency Spreadsheet (SES) and submitted to SAE for feedback.
