MODEL & LOAD CASE

One load case. Several starting designs.

40 × 20Q4 mesh · 800 elements
4 × 2 mDesign domain
0.2 mDensity filter radius
p = 3SIMP penalization

Steel: E = 210 GPa, ν = 0.30; thickness = 0.025 m. Material density is bounded between 0.01 and 1.00. Maximum downward displacement at the loaded point: 1 mm.

THE APPROACH

From stiffness matrix to optimizer.

01 / Planar FEA

I built a four-node plane-stress element solver, assembled a sparse stiffness matrix, and used 2 × 2 Gauss integration.

02 / Filtering

I used a distance-based, normalized density filter to smooth the element densities and reduce checkerboarding.

03 / Sensitivities

I implemented gradients for the objective and displacement constraint, including an adjoint calculation.

04 / Optimization

I used MATLAB fmincon to minimize material cost while enforcing a 1 mm downward displacement limit.
